# Metric Definitions

All formulas apply to aggregate reporting. Zero denominator values display as zero in this demonstration, but should be interpreted as not calculable—not as evidence of zero performance.

- **CTR (%)** = clicks ÷ impressions × 100.
- **CPC (₹)** = advertising spend ÷ clicks.
- **Cost per enquiry (₹)** = advertising spend ÷ enquiries.
- **Cost per qualified lead (₹)** = advertising spend ÷ qualified leads.
- **Qualified lead rate (%)** = qualified leads ÷ enquiries × 100.
- **Appointment show rate (%)** = recorded store visits ÷ appointments × 100. This is a simplified proxy: real clients should distinguish booked appointments, completed appointments and walk-ins.
- **ROAS (×)** = attributed revenue entered in the report ÷ advertising spend. ROAS is not gross margin, profit, or cash received.

The dashboard does not attempt to estimate incrementality, model attribution, VAT/GST, refunds, gross margin, cancelled orders or delayed offline purchases. These must be handled in a real reporting definition with the retailer.
