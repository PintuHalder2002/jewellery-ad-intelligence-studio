# Creative Sample Notes

The three PNGs in `creative_samples/` are original vector-style advertising concept mockups for portfolio demonstration. They do not depict actual products and are not client ads.

1. `creative_01_festive.png` — collection-discovery angle.
2. `creative_02_wedding.png` — occasion / gifting angle.
3. `creative_03_store_visit.png` — store-visit / WhatsApp enquiry angle.

For real campaigns, use high-quality product photos or video supplied by the business, verify usage permission, and get approval for every product description, price, offer, purity/certification statement and call to action. Replace the vector illustrations with actual product assets only after approval. The visuals themselves are deliberately generic so they cannot be mistaken for evidence of a real client's products.
