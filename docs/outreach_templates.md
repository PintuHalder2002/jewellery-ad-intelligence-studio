# Jewellery Retailer Outreach Templates

Personalize every message. Do not send false claims about advertising performance or imply the business has a problem you have not verified.

## First message

Hi [Name], I came across [Business] and liked [specific product / collection / post].

I am building a focused service for jewellery retailers around campaign creatives, WhatsApp enquiry flow and tracking from enquiry to appointment or store visit. I prepared a brief concept for [specific collection or season] and can share a one-page outline.

Would you be open to a 10–15 minute discussion this week?

## Follow-up (after 2–3 business days)

Hi [Name], just following up on my note. The idea is to make it easier to see which campaign messages produce relevant enquiries and store-visit requests—not just likes. Happy to send the one-page outline here. If it is not a priority right now, no problem.

## Discovery questions

1. Which collection or product category do you want to promote first?
2. What is your target geography or ecommerce delivery area?
3. What are your approximate selling price and margin ranges?
4. What campaign or enquiry activity have you tried before?
5. Who responds to enquiries, and how are appointments / sales recorded?
6. What ad spend could you test without putting business cash flow under pressure?

## Offer boundaries

- The retailer pays advertising spend directly through its own account.
- Service fee, deliverables, deadlines and exclusions are written down before work begins.
- No guaranteed leads, sales or ROAS.
- Do not ask for passwords; request appropriate account permissions.
- Do not use customer data or creative assets without permission.
